01 The Builder Compact: Building with Integrity

Canopy is an operating collective for builders who value shipping over posture. By entering the Match directory or claiming an active sprint seat, you agree to:

  • Engage with committed weekly availability as stated on your builder profile.
  • Maintain honest, unvarnished communication during 7-day sprint milestones.
  • Give open attribution to co-builders, upstream libraries, and field mentors.

02 Authentic Participation & Proof of Work

To protect our sprint teams from automated spam and dishonest profiles, all participants must provide verifiable proof of work (such as an authentic GitHub profile, research publication record, or active portfolio).

Zero Tolerance for Sybil Accounts: Accounts created using deceptive identities, stolen credentials, or automated scrapers will be immediately revoked alongside any associated sandbox tokens.

03 Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

The software, firmware schematics, CAD models, and written methodologies created during Canopy sprints belong to the individuals and teams who create them.

Default Permissive Licensing: Unless an explicit commercial agreement is co-signed between a team and a Problem Holder sponsor prior to sprint day one, all codebases submitted to the sprint directory are licensed under standard permissive licenses (MIT or Apache 2.0).

Canopy itself claims zero equity, zero patent rights, and zero royalties from the solutions built by our community.

04 Problem Holder Obligations & Pledges

Civic organizations, researchers, and enterprise teams who publish Build Calls on Canopy commit to the following standards:

Commitment Standard
Lawful Data Any domain datasets provided to sprint teams must be lawfully gathered and scrubbed of sensitive PII.
Timely Feedback Sponsors agree to designate a technical contact to answer team questions within 48 hours during sprint cycles.
Pilot Grants If a Build Call advertises pilot stipends or compute credits, they must be disbursed according to the agreed milestone criteria.

05 Enabler Resources & Non-Dilutive Capital

Enablers who contribute grants, hardware access, or cloud credits do so on a non-dilutive basis unless a formal outside term sheet is separately executed by the sprint team. Canopy does not broker equity exchanges or charge transaction cuts on micro-grants.

06 Code of Conduct & Psychological Safety

We enforce a welcoming environment for builders of all backgrounds, geographic regions, and skill disciplines. Harassment, condescension, plagiarism, or discriminatory behavior will result in immediate removal from sprint channels and revocation of platform membership.

07 Field Reality & Experimental Risk

Canopy sprints produce rapid prototypes, pilot software, and experimental hardware rigs designed to solve difficult real-world challenges.

All solutions are provided on an "as-is" basis. Teams deploying prototypes into live physical environments (such as water sensors, solar inverters, or medical tooling) assume responsibility for thorough safety verification, field calibrations, and compliance with local municipal codes.
