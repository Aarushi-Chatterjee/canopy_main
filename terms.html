Terms and Conditions

Last updated: September 2026. The operating agreements governing sprints, submissions, and code ownership.

1. The Builder Compact

By joining Canopy sprints, you commit to honest, constructive collaboration. Teams agree to execute within designated sprint cycles with transparent communication, real status checkpoints, and open attribution of technical contributions.

2. Intellectual Property and Code Ownership

Unless explicitly negotiated otherwise with a Problem Holder sponsor prior to sprint kickoff, all code, documentation, and hardware schematics generated by builders remain the intellectual property of the respective creators under standard permissive open source licenses (such as MIT or Apache 2.0).

3. Problem Holder Obligations

Organizations and labs posting Build Calls warrant that any underlying datasets provided to collaborators are lawfully collected and comply with local data protection regulations. Promised pilot grants and resource stipends must be honored upon delivery of agreed milestones.

4. Code of Conduct and Safety

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, intellectual theft, or dishonest project misrepresentation will result in immediate suspension of account privileges and removal from active sprint directories.
