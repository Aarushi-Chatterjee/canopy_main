01 The Core Tenet: Zero Data Brokerage

Canopy operates as an open-coordination platform for serious builders, mission-driven problem holders, and catalytic enablers. We are not an advertising network, a lead-generation farm, or an audience data broker.

The Ground Rule: We never sell, rent, monetize, or barter your email address, profile data, match history, or sprint code to third parties. We do not integrate surveillance pixels, behavioral trackers, or retargeting scripts.

Every byte of data we request exists solely to verify authentic participants, establish cross-disciplinary project teams, and document transparent sprint deliverables.

02 Information We Collect & Why

We collect the minimum information required to facilitate collaborative building and prevent spam:

Data Point Source Operational Purpose
Identity & Email Registration or magic link sign-in Authentication, critical sprint updates, match handshakes.
Builder Credentials Match Sandbox & Application Domain skills, GitHub handle, proof-of-work links, time availability.
Build Call Briefs Post a Call submission Field problem statements, pilot scope, funding support availability.
Lab Notebook Notes Open contributions Public field logs, technical post-mortems, open-source code references.

03 Telemetry, Cookies, and Analytics

We respect your browser environment:

No Third-Party Ad Cookies: We do not set commercial advertising cookies. The only client-side storage keys we utilize are essential functional keys (such as canopy_theme for your light/dark display preference and canopy_pass for active session tokens).

Privacy-Preserving Aggregate Metrics: If aggregated site analytics are collected, we use lightweight, cookieless, self-hosted telemetry that complies with GDPR and ePrivacy directives. We do not fingerprint devices or log IP addresses in permanent stores.

04 Code, Schematics, and Artifact Ownership

Code written during Canopy sprints belongs to the people who create it.

Default Stance: Canopy claims no proprietary ownership or commercial license over repositories, CAD schematics, ML checkpoints, or research documentation created by builders or problem holders.

Unless explicitly agreed in a dedicated sponsor grant prior to sprint kickoff, all sprint deliverables are published under permissive open-source licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, or CERN Open Hardware) to maximize public good.

05 Peer Contact Disclosure: The Two-Way Handshake

In the Match Sandbox and directory, builder profiles and problem briefs are listed with verified attributes and public summaries.

To prevent unsolicited solicitation, personal email addresses and direct messaging handles are never broadcast publicly. Contact channels are only revealed once both parties (for instance, Elena R. and the Rural Water Alliance) accept a mutual match handshake.

06 Your Rights: Full Export and Cryptographic Purge

You retain absolute autonomy over your account records:

  • Complete Data Export: You can request a clean JSON bundle of your profile, applications, and sprint history at any time.
  • Right to Deletion: Upon email request to privacy@canopy.earth, we will delete your credentials and profile records from our primary databases within 48 hours.
  • Public Notebook Contributions: Publicly submitted open-source notes in the Lab Notebook may remain in public archives with your name unlinked or replaced by an anonymous contributor tag.

07 Database Breach Resistance, Cryptographic Hashing & Developer Dignity

We treat every builder, problem holder, and enabler with human respect rather than as commoditized platform inventory. Our technical infrastructure is engineered with defense-in-depth security to ensure that personal credentials, private peer handshakes, and project data remain resilient against database breaches:

  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Plaintext passwords never touch our databases. User passwords are salted with high-entropy cryptographic random bytes and derived using the memory-hard scrypt key-derivation algorithm. In the event of a raw database dump or storage leak, credentials cannot be reversed into plain text.
  • Kernel-Level Row Level Security (RLS): Our PostgreSQL architecture strictly enforces database-level Row Level Security policies. Even if public API keys or endpoints are interrogated, the database engine prohibits unauthorized access. Builders can only read and modify their own accounts, and contact emails remain masked until both parties accept a match.
  • Encryption in Transit (TLS 1.3): All network traffic between your browser, our API gateway, and our database cluster is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS). Session tokens and payloads cannot be inspected or intercepted over public networks.
  • Encryption at Rest (AES-256): Underlying database volumes, backups, and physical storage clusters are encrypted using hardware-accelerated AES-256 encryption.
  • Human-Stewarded Custody: Direct administrative access to infrastructure is restricted strictly to designated project stewards using hardware-backed multi-factor authentication. We never grant algorithmic data scrapers, automated brokers, or third-party marketing networks access to our datastores.

If you identify a security or privacy vulnerability in any Canopy service, please disclose it responsibly to privacy@canopy.earth. We respond to all technical disclosures within 24 hours.
