**Platform:** LinkedIn (Feed-Optimized)  
**Format:** 1:1 Square (1080×1080)  
**Duration:** 18.0s (36 beats @ 120 BPM)  
**Pacing:** Kinetic, snappy, punchy, mute-friendly  
**Aesthetic:** Bold Editorial Light Mode (Warm Ivory `#FAF7F0`, Deep Pine `#0F2F20`, Neon Sprout `#34A853`, Terracotta `#A9321A`, Gold Amber `#D48806`)

**Anchor Asset:** Mascot Squad Banner (Dev, Blueprint Architect, Researcher, Growth Lead)

- **Visuals:** High-contrast statement card spotlighting the official 4-mascot banner with kinetic badges:
