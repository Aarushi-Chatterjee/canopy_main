# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Canopy (LinkedIn Edition)

## Objective
Create a short, polished, beautiful 18-second launch promo video for Canopy in 1:1 Square (1080×1080) for LinkedIn.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output-linkedin/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output-linkedin/brag.mp4`
- Poster frame: `brag-output-linkedin/brag.jpg`
- Format: square — 1080x1080 @ 30fps
- Duration: 18.0 seconds (540 frames)

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "Warm, vibrant, botanical light-mode product film tailored for LinkedIn innovators and builders."
- Angle: Ambitious products fail when built alone in silos. Canopy matches builders and domain experts into high-velocity 14-day sprints.
- Visual hook: The four cute sprout mascots (Dev, Blueprint Architect, Researcher, Growth) working under the lush canopy tree branch.
- Climax / outro: "Build. Connect. Ship."

## Visual Identity
- Background: `#FAF7EE` (warm light-mode textured botanical parchment)
- Surface Cards: `#FFFFFF` and `#FDFBF7` with fine organic borders (`rgba(31, 92, 64, 0.12)`)
- Forest Green Ink: `#1A4D36`
- Botanical Leaf Green: `#4E9B44`
- Terracotta / Coral: `#E06D53` (from mascot banner & tagline)
- Sun Gold: `#E2AE38`
- Display font: `Fraunces`, Georgia, serif
- Body font: `Jost`, -apple-system, sans-serif

## Audio
- Audio track: `assets/track.mp3` (120 BPM upbeat groove)
- Audio treatment: Clean build, rhythm driving product showcases, triumphant finish.
